Your instructor

Darrell Thompson

Founder, Right Side of Money

I grew up on the South Side of Chicago, dropped out of high school, and joined the Marine Corps in 1988. Nobody handed me a playbook for money. I learned it the hard way, then I learned it the right way: two degrees, a career in federal service, licenses in insurance and real estate, and years of studying how the financial system really works.

I wrote Creating A Wealth Building Mindset 2nd Edition and built Right Side of Money for the people the financial system was never designed to teach. This program is where that education starts. It isn't about getting rich quick. It's about learning the game so you can finally play it on purpose.
