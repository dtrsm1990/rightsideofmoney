Level 3 · Module 3 · Weeks 11 to 14 · Scoreboard and Lock-In

The Legacy Scoreboard

This final scoreboard confirms that your wealth can survive without you: values written, competence being transferred, governance in place, and structures matching your intentions.

Review: Darrell verifies every scoreboard personally

The Last Failure Point

Most wealth that survives one generation fails in the next, because heirs inherit assets without systems. This scoreboard proves you've started transferring the systems: the statement, the binder, the readiness plan, the council, and the transfer map.

Total Accountability

When this scoreboard is verified, you've proven control of your foundation, your architecture, your protection, and your legacy. That's Total Accountability: nothing left unexplained, nothing left to chance, and nothing that depends on luck.

Before You Submit

Your Worksheets and Daily Logs

Everything below is attached to your scoreboard automatically. Every worksheet has to be complete before you can submit.

    Step 1

    The Scoreboard

    Check each item only if it's complete and you're submitting the proof for it. All 13 items are required. Partial completion isn't accepted.

    0 of 13 complete

    Step 2

    Re-Measure

    Same measures as your baseline, measured today. Your last recorded numbers are shown in gold under each field so you can see the change, but enter today's truth.

    Cash Flow, Reserve, and Debt

    Average of your last three months, after taxes and deductions, from every source.
    Money saved, invested (including payroll retirement contributions), or paid toward debt above the minimums.
    Emergency fund and savings you could reach within a few days. Not retirement accounts.
    Housing, utilities, food, transportation, insurance, and minimum debt payments.
    Required payments on cards, car loans, student loans, personal loans, and BNPL. Not your mortgage.
    A full review of your numbers and decisions against your rules. At baseline, count any real weekly money review you held.

    Net Worth

    Retirement accounts, brokerage accounts, and other investments, at their current value.
    Everything you own: cash, invested assets, home value, vehicles, business equity.
    Everything you owe, including your mortgage.
    Things that, if they broke, would take everything down: one income, one person who knows everything, one account holding it all.

    Protections in Place

    Check only what's actually in place and current. Most people check few of these at baseline. That's why they're here.

    Governance and Continuity in Place

    Total Accountability Mindset

    Rate each statement honestly. 1 means strongly disagree, 5 means strongly agree.

    1 Strongly disagree5 Strongly agree

    1. I know who controls every asset I own, and who would control it if I couldn't.

    2. My household money decisions follow written rules, even under pressure.

    3. I can say no to money requests that break our policy, without guilt.

    4. I know exactly what would happen to my family financially if I died or couldn't work.

    5. I've faced the documents and decisions most people avoid.

    6. The people who will inherit from me are being prepared, not just provided for.

    7. I review my wealth on a schedule, even when nothing seems wrong.

    8. My wealth could keep working if I were gone.

    • Proof of professional appointments for any transfer gaps
    • Anything else that supports a worksheet answer
