Module 3 Assessment

Financial Data Literacy Quiz

This quiz proves you can work the numbers, then puts your own numbers to work. You'll receive a personalized analysis of your cash flow, savings rate, emergency runway, interest cost, and your primary financial pressure point, with specific 90-day targets. Have your Core 8 page and your Leak Audit in front of you. Plan for 30 to 40 minutes.

Participant Information

Your report is prepared under this name and sent to this email address and to Darrell.

Part 1: Calculation and Knowledge

Ten real-world calculation and scenario questions. You need 80% (8 of 10) to unlock Module 4. A calculator is allowed and encouraged.

1Darnell takes home $4,600 a month and pays $1,650 in rent. About what share of his take-home goes to housing?

2Sonia has a $12,000 credit card balance at 19.9% APR. What's her approximate monthly interest cost?

3Lamar takes home $5,000 a month and automatically saves $350. What's his savings rate?

4Janelle has $6,000 in emergency savings and $3,000 in monthly essential expenses. What's her emergency runway?

5Kim owns a car worth $12,000, has $21,000 in retirement savings and $5,000 in cash. She owes $46,000 in student loans and car debt. What's her net worth?

6Over the last six months, Troy's commission income was $3,100, $4,800, $3,900, $5,200, $3,300, and $4,400. What should he use as his monthly planning baseline?

7Rita pays for four subscriptions: $12.99, $15.49, $9.99, and $17.99 a month. What's the approximate annual cost?

8Victor's minimum debt payments are a $450 car payment, $300 on credit cards, and $150 on student loans. He takes home $4,500. What's his debt payment ratio?

9During a Calm Review, Deborah finds these Red items: a water shutoff notice, a department store card 60 days late, and a gym membership in collections. Using essentials-first triage, what comes first?

10Using the lesson's illustration, a $200 monthly leak redirected and invested at a hypothetical 7% average annual return for 10 years could grow to roughly how much?
