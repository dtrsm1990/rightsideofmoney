Why Level 3 Exists

Wealth Doesn't Fail From a Lack of Money.

It fails from a lack of structure. A lawsuit nobody planned for. A disability nobody insured against. A business partner with no written agreement. A spouse who doesn't know where anything is. Heirs who inherit money but none of the discipline that built it. Most family wealth is gone by the third generation, and it's almost never because they didn't earn enough.

Level 3 closes every one of those gaps. You'll start by proving your foundation is solid, then write down exactly who owns what, who decides what, and who has access to what. You'll stress-test your protection against real scenarios, build your document and beneficiary map, and prepare the people who come after you to receive wealth without being ruined by it.

Level 3 stands on its own. If you haven't done Levels 1 and 2, the Foundation weeks cover the essentials you need. Every phase ends with a scoreboard Darrell reviews personally, and progress is measured by the Total Accountability Index and your net worth, from baseline to graduation.

Is Level 3 Right for You?

Built for People Who Want Nothing Left to Chance.

Level 3 is for you if

  • You've built something, or you're building it, and you want it controlled, protected, and passed on
  • You have a family, a business, property, or people who depend on your decisions
  • You don't know who would decide for you, or what would happen, if you couldn't act tomorrow
  • You want your heirs ready to receive wealth, not ruined by it
  • You want proof, not another seminar

Level 3 isn't for you if

  • You're living paycheck to paycheck or missing payments (start with Level 1)
  • You want someone to draft your will or sell you a policy
  • You aren't willing to put your rules and your plan in writing and submit evidence
  • You want someone to remind you and chase you
Level 3 stands on its own. You don't have to complete Levels 1 or 2 first. The Foundation weeks cover what you need, and your application is reviewed before enrollment. Level 2 graduates who move up within 60 days receive their full $2,497 Level 2 investment as credit toward Level 3.
