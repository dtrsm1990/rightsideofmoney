Part 2: Day 1 Knowledge Check

Ten real-life scenarios. You need 80% (8 of 10) to unlock Day 2.

1Which is the best description of the difference between saving and investing?

2Using the Rule of 72, about how long does money take to double at 6% a year?

3Two people invest $200 a month until 65 at the same hypothetical return. One starts at 25, one at 35. What mostly explains the large difference in results?

4Keisha keeps $20,000 in a checking account earning nothing for 20 years while inflation averages 3%. What happens?

5Which statement about risk and return is true?

6The market drops 25% in two months. Marcus is investing for retirement in 30 years. Based on this lesson, what's usually the most costly reaction?

7What is 'behavior risk'?

8Denise has $4,000 on a credit card at 23% APR and no emergency fund. Her employer offers no match. What's usually the wiser first move before investing?

9Kevin needs $15,000 for a home down payment in 18 months. According to the five-year guideline, where does that money generally belong?

10Someone online promises you 30% returns in six months with no risk if you send money today. What does this lesson say?

Part 3: Investing Mindset Diagnostic

How much do you agree with each statement?

1.The idea of losing money in the market scares me enough to keep me from starting.
2.I feel safer keeping all my money in cash, even if it loses value to inflation.
3.I could stay invested through a market drop.
4.Investing feels like something for people with more money or education than me.
5.The terms and options make me feel overwhelmed.
6.I believe I can learn to invest well.
7.I'm drawn to investments that promise big, fast gains.
8.I'd consider putting money into a hot tip from social media or a friend.
9.I understand that steady, long-term investing builds more wealth than chasing quick wins.
10.I want to see results quickly or I lose interest.
11.I would check my investments often and react to daily changes.
12.I can commit to investing for 10 years or more.
