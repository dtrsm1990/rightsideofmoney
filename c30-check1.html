Day 7 | Week 1 Progress Check

Week 1 Progress Check: Awareness and Foundations

Seven days in. This check reviews your Week 1 knowledge, identifies your primary spending driver, and pulls in your tracker, affirmation, and money move. Plan for 15 to 20 minutes.

Participant Information

Part 1: Knowledge Check

Five real-life scenarios from Week 1. You need 80% (4 of 5) to pass and unlock Week 2.

1Rashad logs a $5 snack but skips recording his feeling because it "doesn't matter." Why does the feeling matter?

2Which affirmation best follows the RSM formula?

3Tia shops after arguments with her boyfriend to feel better. Which driver and fix match?

4Omar takes home $22 an hour and spends $44 on delivery twice a week. How many life hours is that per week?

5Why is an automatic transfer more effective than saving what's left at the end of the month?

Part 2: Spending Driver Diagnostic

Choose how often each statement was true for you this week.

1.I spent money after a stressful moment to feel better.
2.Stressful days led to purchases I wouldn't normally make.
3.I bought something to feel a sense of control when things felt chaotic.
4.When I felt stressed, I used a non-spending way to calm down.
5.I browsed shopping apps or sites when I had nothing else to do.
6.Scrolling led me to buy something I wasn't looking for.
7.I shopped late at night out of habit.
8.I had enjoyable free activities to turn to when I was bored.
9.I told myself "I deserve this" before an unplanned purchase.
10.My spending went up on or right after payday.
11.After doing well, I celebrated by spending more than I planned.
12.My rewards this week were planned in advance.
13.I ordered delivery or takeout because I didn't have a plan.
14.I paid extra for speed or ease without thinking about it.
15.I made convenience store or drive-thru stops I could have avoided.
16.I planned meals or errands ahead to avoid convenience costs.
