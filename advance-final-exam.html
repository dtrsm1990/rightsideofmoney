Case Study: The Reed Household

Marcus (38, logistics coordinator) and Tanya (36, pharmacy technician) Reed have two children. They take home $6,400 a month combined.

Monthly ItemAmount
Rent$2,050
Two car payments$780

Discretionary (includes $520 delivery and takeout, $140 in subscriptions)$1,420
Minimum payments on credit cards and student loans$430
Money Tanya sends to her mother and brother$300
Total outflow$6,720

They owe $14,300 on credit cards at about 24% APR and $22,000 in student loans at 6%. They have $600 in savings and save nothing automatically.

Tanya grew up as the oldest child who helped raise her siblings. She says, "If I have it and they need it, how can I say no?" Marcus hasn't opened a credit card statement in four months. He says it "just stresses him out." He often shops online after 11 p.m. following long shifts. They argue about money about once a week, usually when a bill is late.
