Step 3 Guide

Set Up Your Amazon KDP Account.

Complete this before your launch session. It takes about 30 to 45 minutes, one time. This is your account: you own it, you control it, and Amazon pays your royalties directly to you.

Have these ready before you start

  • Your Amazon login. Amazon recommends using your existing Amazon customer account so ordering proof copies works smoothly later.
  • Your legal name, physical street address (no PO boxes), and phone number
  • Your bank routing number and account number for direct deposit
  • Your Social Security number or business EIN for the tax interview
  • A government-issued photo ID, in case Amazon asks you to verify your identity
  • Your cell phone, for two-step verification codes
  1. Sign in. Go to kdp.amazon.com and sign in with your Amazon account. No Amazon account? Click Sign up and create one.
  2. Accept the terms. Read and accept the KDP Terms and Conditions.
  3. Open your account. Click Your Account at the top of the page and complete two-step verification.
  4. Account details. Enter your legal name, address, and phone. Don't enter a pen name here. Amazon pays you and issues tax forms under this name. A pen name gets added later, on the book itself.
  5. Getting paid. Add your bank account for direct deposit. It pays royalties with no minimum threshold and is more secure than paper checks.
  6. Tax information. Click Complete Tax Profile and answer every question. You can't publish until your status shows "Complete."
  7. Identity verification. If Amazon asks, upload clear photos of your ID. The name must match your account name.
  8. Let us know. Text or email: "My KDP account is set up and my tax profile says Complete." Then we'll book your launch session.
Good to know

Amazon sends you a 1099 if you earn at least $10 in royalties in a year. Keep it for your taxes. Never share your password or verification codes with anyone, including us. Stuck? Take a screenshot of the screen and send it to us.

Source: Amazon KDP Help, Set Up Your KDP Account and Manage Your KDP Account.

Step 4 Guide

File Requirements.

Upload everything to your private shared folder. Clean files now mean a faster, smoother project.

ItemFormatWhat we need
ManuscriptWord (.docx)Final, edited text in one file. No text boxes, fancy fonts, or page breaks made by pressing Enter.
Front matterIn the manuscriptDedication, acknowledgments, and foreword, if you have them.
Back matterIn the manuscriptAbout the author, how to reach you, other books, and a call to action.
Images and chartsJPG or PNG, 300 DPIEach as its own file, named in order (image-01, image-02). Mark in the manuscript where each one goes.
Author photoJPG, high resolutionA clear, well-lit headshot for your back cover and Amazon author page.
Author bioWord or plain textA 3 to 5 sentence version and a 1 sentence version.
Cover ideasLinks or screenshotsCovers you love, colors you want, and anything to avoid.
PermissionsPDF or emailWritten permission for any quotes, lyrics, or photos you didn't create.

Before you upload, check

  • Spell-check is run and every chapter is in final order
  • Chapter titles are consistent, like all "Chapter 1: Title" or all "1. Title"
  • Every image is something you own or have permission to use
  • You've read the whole thing one last time. After formatting starts, text changes cost time and may cost extra.
