Student Roster

Everyone enrolled in your courses and Accountability levels, who applied but hasn't enrolled, and every quiz lead, built from your Formspree exports. Your files are read only inside this browser. Nothing is uploaded or saved anywhere.

How to Update Your Roster

  1. Log in to formspree.io. For each form, open Submissions and choose Export to download a CSV file. Export every form you want included.
  2. Drop all of the CSV files into the box below at once, or tap the box to choose them.
  3. Your roster appears instantly. Use Download Roster to save it as a spreadsheet, or Print to keep a copy.
