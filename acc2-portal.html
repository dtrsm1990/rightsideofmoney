How Level 2 Works

12 Weeks. 3 Modules. 3 Verified Scoreboards.

Level 2 turns participants into operators. Each module runs four weeks and ends with a scoreboard: your numbers, every required framework, your proof logs, and your evidence. Darrell reviews every scoreboard personally. When it's verified, you receive a code that unlocks the next module. If it isn't, you revise and resubmit. Nothing moves forward on a promise.

Every week has a teaching on what to do and why, a Mindset Drill that confronts the thinking behind your decisions, and a Field Assignment built around fill-in worksheets right on the page. You keep a daily proof log here too, so you never build a spreadsheet. Everything saves as you type, collects in your Operator Workbook, and goes to Darrell automatically with your scoreboard.

You close each week with an Operator Log, hold your own 30-minute Weekly Operator Review, and meet with Darrell for a 20-minute one-on-one check-in. After every scoreboard you receive a written Progress Report and Darrell's personal written feedback.

    The Standard

    The Rules of Level 2

    1

    Proof over promises. Every scoreboard requires your frameworks, your logs, and your evidence. Darrell verifies it. If it can't be verified, it didn't happen.

    2

    Modules lock in order. The next module opens only after the last scoreboard is verified. Partial completion isn't accepted, and a scoreboard that fails is repeated.

    3

    Deadlines are posted, not chased. Every deadline is on this page from day one. Nobody is going to remind you. Operators run their own calendar.

    4

    Strikes. A scoreboard (or your Commitment & Baseline) submitted more than 48 hours after its deadline is a strike. Two strikes pauses your program until you submit a recommitment and Darrell reinstates you. When you're reinstated, your remaining deadlines are reset on a fresh schedule, and the next strike pauses you again.

    5

    Report every broken rule. Your weekly log asks whether any decision broke one of your written rules. Reporting it is discipline. Hiding it is the real violation.

    6

    Protect your information. Black out account numbers, your Social Security number, and your full date of birth on everything you upload.

    7

    Education, not advice. Level 2 teaches you to build and enforce your own rules. It doesn't tell you what to buy, sell, or borrow. For decisions about your specific situation, use a licensed professional.

    Your 1:1s With Darrell

    Weekly Check-Ins and Three Strategy Calls

    Every week: a 20-minute one-on-one check-in with Darrell at a standing time you set on your Launch Call. Your Operator Log includes your agenda, so Darrell has read it before you get on the call. Twelve weeks, twelve check-ins.

    At the moments that matter most: three longer strategy calls. Come prepared. These aren't pep talks. They're working sessions.
