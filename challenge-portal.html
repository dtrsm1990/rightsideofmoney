A message from Darrell

Most people don't have a money problem. They have a clarity problem. They don't know what's coming in, what's going out, why they make the decisions they make, or where they want to end up. This challenge fixes that in three days. Not with inspiration alone, but with real work. You're going to audit your financial life, confront the beliefs that have been costing you, and build a framework that actually works. I've worked with people who turned this three-day process into the turning point they'd been waiting years for. You're ready. Let's go.

How to Get the Most From This Challenge

  1. Do the work. Reading gives you knowledge. Doing the exercises gives you change. Don't skip the reflection questions or the scorecard exercises. That's where the real transformation happens.
  2. Be honest. This challenge only works if you put real numbers on the page. No judgment lives here. The truth is the starting point, not the verdict.
  3. Complete each knowledge check before moving on. You need 80% to unlock the next day. That's not a barrier; it's confirmation that the material landed before you build on it.
  4. Come back to this. The framework you build here is meant to be used every month. Return to Day 3 when you need to reset. Return to Day 1 when your situation changes.

What You'll Need

Before you start Day 1, gather these:

  • Your last 3 months of bank and credit card statements (digital or printed)
  • A list of all your monthly bills and obligations
  • A rough idea of everything you own and everything you owe
  • An honest 30 minutes of focused time per day
