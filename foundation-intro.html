Introduction

Why This Course Exists

Before we build anything, you need to understand what we're building and why it matters so much.

The Hard Truth

The financial system in America wasn't designed with you in mind. It was designed to serve those who already understood it, and to profit from those who didn't. That's not an excuse. That's a starting point. Because once you understand that, you stop blaming yourself for what you didn't know, and you start focusing on learning what you need to know right now.

The Story Behind This Course

I grew up on the South Side of Chicago. Nobody in my neighborhood talked about investing, compound interest, or building generational wealth. Money was something you worked for, spent to survive, and never had enough of. That was the script, and most of the people around me followed it their entire lives.

I joined the Marine Corps at 18. The discipline the Corps gave me was invaluable, but the financial education still wasn't there. I made decent money for the first time in my life and did what most young people do without guidance: I spent it. I bought things I thought I deserved, took on debt I didn't understand, and made decisions I spent years correcting.

After the Corps I spent years in federal service, earned two degrees, got into real estate, insurance, and financial education. And somewhere along the way, everything clicked. Not because I got lucky. Because I finally learned the rules of the game I'd been playing blind my entire life.

That's why this course exists. The information in these modules isn't complicated. It isn't secret. But for millions of people, especially from working class and middle class communities, nobody ever sat them down and explained it clearly. This course is that conversation I wish someone had given me at 20. Or 25. Or 30.

You're getting it now. That's what matters.

The Three Pillars of This Course

Pillar 1: Mindset

Everything financial starts between your ears. The beliefs you carry about money, who it's for, whether you deserve it, and what having it would mean about you drive every financial decision you make, often without you realizing it. We address this in Module 1 before we touch a single number, because building financial skills on a broken money mindset is like building a house on sand.

Pillar 2: Financial Literacy

You can't navigate a system you don't understand. Financial literacy isn't about becoming an accountant or a stock trader. It's about knowing enough to make informed decisions, ask the right questions, spot the bad deals, and take advantage of the tools the system already has in place for people who know how to use them. Modules 2 through 7 build this literacy from the ground up.

Pillar 3: Accountability

The biggest gap in most financial education is the gap between knowing and doing. Module 6 addresses accountability directly, but every module includes reflection questions and action steps because information without application is just entertainment. You're not here to be entertained. You're here to change your financial life.

A Few Ground Rules

  1. Do the reflection questions. Every module ends with interactive questions designed to take the concepts off the page and into your actual life. Your answers save automatically in this browser.
  2. Use real numbers. Every exercise asks you to work with your actual financial situation. The concepts work at any income level, but only if you're honest about where you actually are.
  3. Go at your pace, but keep moving. There's no deadline, but there is momentum. Let each module settle before moving on. Don't sit so long that you lose the thread.
  4. Come back. This course is a reference, not a one-time event. The module on debt will hit differently once you've built your budget. Return to these lessons as you level up.
