Why Most Books Never Get Published

The Book Isn't the Problem. The Process Is.

Most people with a book inside them never see it on a shelf. Not because their story doesn't matter, but because nobody showed them the path.

"Your story could be the reason someone else finally believes they can make it. It deserves better than a folder on your desktop."

  • Formatting gets rejected.Margins, trim sizes, fonts, and file types have to be exactly right, or Amazon sends it back.
  • Covers get cut off.A print cover needs a spine, bleed, safe zones, and room for the barcode. Get one wrong and your title gets trimmed.
  • Nobody can find the book.Without the right keywords, categories, and description, a great book sits invisible.
  • Expensive companies take your rights.Some "publishing" companies charge thousands, then keep a cut of your royalties or control of your book.
  • Life gets in the way.Without a plan and someone holding you to it, "someday" never comes.

Questions, Answered

Frequently Asked Questions.

Do I keep the rights to my book and my royalties?
Yes, 100%. You publish under your own Amazon KDP account, you own your copyright and files, and Amazon pays your royalties directly to you. We never take a percentage of your sales.
Do I need my own Amazon account?
Yes, and we'll walk you through setting it up with a simple step-by-step guide. It takes about 30 to 45 minutes, one time. We never ask for your password. On launch day, you log in on your own computer and we guide you live on a video call.
How long does it take?
Most books go live on Amazon within 4 to 6 weeks of receiving your final manuscript and materials. The biggest factor is how quickly approvals go back and forth. Signature Author projects take longer because they include the writing phase.
What if my book isn't finished yet?
That's what the Signature Author package is for. We'll help you outline it, build a writing plan, and keep you accountable until your manuscript is done, then take it all the way to Amazon.
Do you edit my book?
We review every manuscript and tell you honestly what it needs. When professional editing is needed, we coordinate it with trusted editors and quote it separately, so you only pay for what your book actually needs.
Which formats can I publish?
Kindle ebook and paperback are included in every package. Hardcover is included with Signature Author and can be added to any other package.
Do I need to buy an ISBN?
No. Amazon provides a free ISBN for paperbacks and hardcovers. If you'd rather own your ISBN and publish under your own imprint name, we'll explain the difference and help you decide.
Can you guarantee my book will sell?
No honest publishing partner can promise sales, and you should be careful with anyone who does. What we guarantee is a professional book that's set up the right way, with the description, keywords, and launch plan that give it a real chance to be found.
How much does it cost?
Packages start at $997. Your exact price is confirmed on your free discovery call based on your book's length, complexity, and the support you want. No surprises and no hidden fees.
