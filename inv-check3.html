Part 2: Risk Tolerance Check

How much do you agree with each statement? This shapes your risk profile.

1.If my investments dropped 20% in a year, I would stay invested or even invest more.
2.I'm comfortable with bigger ups and downs in exchange for more growth potential over time.
3.Seeing my balance go down would keep me up at night.
4.I have a stable income and emergency savings, so I wouldn't need to sell investments in a downturn.
5.I'd rather have slow, steady growth than risk large short-term losses.
6.I understand that market drops are a normal part of long-term investing.
