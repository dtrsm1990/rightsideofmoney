Your RSM Publishing Services Author Guide

Your secure portal becomes available after your agreement is signed, the required initial payment is verified, and your invitation is authorized. Keep your sign-in details private.

1. Activate your account

Open your invitation email, follow the secure link, and set your password when prompted. Return to this Author Dashboard to access your project. If your invitation has expired, contact publishing@rightsideofmoney.com. Never email your password.

2. Track your publishing progress

Your assigned book displays its current publishing stage. This stage reflects recorded project activity, not a claim that every quality check is complete. Use Refresh Dashboard for the latest released information.

3. Submit requested materials

Use Upload Document within your project when RSM requests a manuscript, supporting source, cover artwork, or revision. RSM preserves the original manuscript and makes edits in a separate working copy.

4. Review editorial corrections

After administrator approval, edited manuscript drafts and reports become available among your authorized project files. Review corrections and concrete improvement recommendations. RSM may apply safe routine copyedits directly, while substantive changes require your decision.

5. Complete author action items

Only approved requests appear in your Author Action Checklist. Read each request and submit the requested approval, exact replacement wording, clarification, or supporting evidence. Your response is recorded for RSM's editorial team.

6. Review proofs and final files

RSM releases proofs and publication materials after internal review and required approvals. Respond to proofing requests promptly. Publication readiness requires completed editorial and production quality checks.

Questions or access problems? Contact publishing@rightsideofmoney.com. Unapproved internal drafts and provisional findings remain private.
