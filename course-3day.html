Common Questions

How long does each day take?

Each day is designed to take one to two hours depending on how deeply you engage with the reflection exercises. You can go faster. But the people who slow down tend to get more out of it.

Do I need to complete it in three consecutive days?

No. You move at your pace. Some people complete it in three days. Others stretch it across a week. The content is self-paced and your progress is saved in your browser.

What if I don't pass the knowledge check?

You can retake each knowledge check as many times as you need. The goal is mastery, not speed. Go back to the lesson, review the section the question pointed you to, and try again.

Is this only for people who are struggling financially?

No. This challenge helps anyone who wants more clarity and intention around their money, whether they're in debt, breaking even, or already earning well but not building wealth the way they could.
